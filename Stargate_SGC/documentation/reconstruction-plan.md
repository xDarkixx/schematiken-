# SGC reconstruction plan

## Priority areas

1. Cheyenne Mountain exterior and entrance
2. Level 28 Stargate room and gate shaft
3. Level 28 operations/control room
4. Level 27 briefing room
5. Level 27 commander's office
6. elevator/stair connections
7. corridors and security checkpoints
8. remaining underground levels and support areas

## Accuracy rule

The project is a reconstruction rather than a direct conversion of a fan-made Minecraft world. Existing maps can help establish scale and connectivity, but show/production references are used to correct visible rooms and set geometry.

## PneumaticCraft

Elevator shafts are to remain open and vertically continuous. Elevator frames/bases are not to be embedded into solid floors. Floor openings must leave sufficient clearance for PneumaticCraft elevator components.

## Validation before final release

- NBT schematic structure valid
- dimensions checked
- block IDs limited to Minecraft 1.7.10-compatible IDs unless explicitly documented as modded
- no accidental solid blocks in elevator shafts
- major rooms and corridors are walkable
- Level 27/28 connections are continuous
- exterior entrance connects to the underground complex
